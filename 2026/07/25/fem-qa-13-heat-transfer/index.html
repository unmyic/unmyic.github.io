L14 — Heat Transfer（热传导）🟡 样卷未考

Q108 🟡 Fourier热传导定律

EN: Fourier’s law of heat conduction in 1D:

qx=−KxxdTdx\boxed{q_x = -K_{xx}\frac{dT}{dx}} qx​=−Kxx​dxdT​​

where qxq_xqx​ is the heat flux (kW/m²), KxxK_{xx}Kxx​ is the thermal conductivity (W/m/K), and dT/dxdT/dxdT/dx is the temperature gradient. The negative sign indicates heat flows from hot to cold (down the temperature gradient).

中文： Fourier定律：热流 qxq_xqx​ = −导热系数 × 温度梯度。热量从高温流向低温（负号）。K的单位是W/m/K。这是热传导的基本定律。

Q109 🟡 稳态热传导方程

EN: Steady-state heat conduction (no time dependence, dT/dt=0dT/dt = 0dT/dt=0):

1D: Kxxd2Tdx2+Q=0K_{xx}\frac{d^2T}{dx^2} + Q = 0Kxx​dx2d2T​+Q=0

2D (orthotropic): Kxx∂2T∂x2+Kyy∂2T∂y2+Q=0K_{xx}\frac{\partial^2T}{\partial x^2} + K_{yy}\frac{\partial^2T}{\partial y^2} + Q = 0Kxx​∂x2∂2T​+Kyy​∂y2∂2T​+Q=0

where QQQ is the internal heat generation rate per unit volume (kW/m³).

中文： 稳态（dT/dt=0dT/dt=0dT/dt=0）热传导方程——1D：KxxT′′+Q=0K_{xx}T''+Q=0Kxx​T′′+Q=0；2D：KxxT,xx+KyyT,yy+Q=0K_{xx}T_{,xx}+K_{yy}T_{,yy}+Q=0Kxx​T,xx​+Kyy​T,yy​+Q=0。Q是内热源。注意与结构力学平衡方程的类比：TTT（温度）↔ uuu（位移）。

Q110 🟡 热传导的FEM基本变量

EN: In heat transfer FEM, the primary unknown is temperature TTT (analogous to displacement uuu in structural FEM). The element equation has the same form:

keTd=Pe\mathbf{k}_e \mathbf{T}_d = \mathbf{P}_e ke​Td​=Pe​

where ke\mathbf{k}_eke​ = element conductivity matrix (analogous to stiffness matrix), Td\mathbf{T}_dTd​ = nodal temperature vector, Pe\mathbf{P}_ePe​ = element thermal load vector (internal heat generation + surface heat flux).

中文： 热传导FEM的基本变量是温度T（类比结构FEM的位移u）。单元方程形式相同：keTd=Pe\mathbf{k}_e\mathbf{T}_d=\mathbf{P}_eke​Td​=Pe​，"刚度矩阵"变成热传导矩阵，"力"变成热载荷。

Q111 🟡 热传导B矩阵的作用

EN: In heat transfer FEM, the B-matrix maps nodal temperatures to temperature gradients:

{∂T/∂x∂T/∂y}=BTd\begin{Bmatrix} \partial T/\partial x \\ \partial T/\partial y \end{Bmatrix} = \mathbf{B} \mathbf{T}_d {∂T/∂x∂T/∂y​}=BTd​

This is analogous to the strain-displacement B-matrix in structural FEM: ε=Bd\boldsymbol{\varepsilon} = \mathbf{Bd}ε=Bd. The conductivity (stiffness) matrix is ke=∫hBTKB dxdy\mathbf{k}_e = \int h\mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{K}\mathbf{B}\,dxdyke​=∫hBTKBdxdy, where K=[Kxx00Kyy]\mathbf{K} = \begin{bmatrix}K_{xx}&0\\0&K_{yy}\end{bmatrix}K=[Kxx​0​0Kyy​​] is the conductivity matrix.

中文： 热传导的B矩阵将节点温度映射为温度梯度（类比结构FEM的应变-位移B矩阵）。ke=∫hBTKB dxdy\mathbf{k}_e=\int h\mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{K}\mathbf{B}\,dxdyke​=∫hBTKBdxdy，K是导热系数矩阵。

Q112 🟡 热传导边界条件

EN: Two types of boundary conditions in heat transfer:

  1. Specified temperature (Dirichlet): T=TˉT = \bar{T}T=Tˉ on S1S_1S1​ (analogous to displacement BC)
  2. Specified heat flux (Neumann): qn=Kxx∂T∂xnx+Kyy∂T∂ynyq_n = K_{xx}\frac{\partial T}{\partial x}n_x + K_{yy}\frac{\partial T}{\partial y}n_yqn​=Kxx​∂x∂T​nx​+Kyy​∂y∂T​ny​ on S2S_2S2​ (analogous to traction BC)

中文： 两类热边界——(1)指定温度（Dirichlet，类比位移BC）；(2)指定热流（Neumann，类比力BC）。热流 = 导热系数 × 温度梯度 · 法向量。

Q113 🟡 热传导和结构FEM的类比

EN: The FEM procedures for heat transfer and structural analysis are completely analogous:

Structural Heat Transfer
Displacement uuu Temperature TTT
Strain ε=Bd\boldsymbol{\varepsilon} = \mathbf{Bd}ε=Bd Temperature gradient ∇T=BTd\nabla T = \mathbf{BT}_d∇T=BTd​
Stress σ=Dε\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \mathbf{D}\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}σ=Dε Heat flux q=−K∇T\mathbf{q} = -\mathbf{K}\nabla Tq=−K∇T
Stiffness k=∫BTDB dV\mathbf{k} = \int\mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{D}\mathbf{B}\,dVk=∫BTDBdV Conductivity k=∫BTKB dV\mathbf{k} = \int\mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{K}\mathbf{B}\,dVk=∫BTKBdV
Force F\mathbf{F}F Thermal load P\mathbf{P}P

The same element types, shape functions, isoparametric formulation, Jacobian, Gauss integration, and assembly procedures are used.

中文： 结构和热传导FEM完全类比——位移↔温度、应变↔温度梯度、应力↔热流、刚度矩阵↔传导矩阵。相同的单元类型、形函数、等参变换、Gauss积分都可以直接使用。这是FEM扩展到多物理场的基础。

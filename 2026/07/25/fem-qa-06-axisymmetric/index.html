L8 — Axisymmetric Elements（轴对称单元）

Q43 🔴 轴对称的四个必要条件

EN: For a problem to be treated as axisymmetric, ALL four conditions must be satisfied:

  1. Geometry — the structure is a solid of revolution (rotationally symmetric about an axis)
  2. Material properties — axisymmetric (same in all circumferential directions)
  3. Boundary conditions — axisymmetric (no variation in θ\thetaθ)
  4. Loading — axisymmetric (no variation in θ\thetaθ)

If any condition is violated, a full 3D or other simplified model must be used.

中文： 轴对称必须同时满足几何、材料、边界条件、载荷四个方面的轴对称性。任何一个不满足都不能简化为轴对称模型。典型结构：轴、管道、压力容器、回转体。

Q44 🟡 轴对称坐标系统

EN: Axisymmetric problems use cylindrical coordinates (r,θ,z)(r, \theta, z)(r,θ,z). Due to axisymmetry:

  • ∂(⋅)∂θ=0\frac{\partial(\cdot)}{\partial\theta} = 0∂θ∂(⋅)​=0 — nothing varies with circumferential angle
  • vθ=0v_\theta = 0vθ​=0 — no circumferential displacement
  • Displacements are functions of rrr and zzz only: u=u(r,z)u = u(r,z)u=u(r,z), w=w(r,z)w = w(r,z)w=w(r,z)

中文： 柱坐标 (r,θ,z)(r,\theta,z)(r,θ,z)。轴对称条件：∂(⋅)/∂θ=0\partial(\cdot)/\partial\theta=0∂(⋅)/∂θ=0（无不随θ变化），vθ=0v_\theta=0vθ​=0（无环向位移）。位移仅为r和z的函数，问题从3D降为2D（r-z平面）。

Q45 ⭐ 环向应变 εθ=u/r\varepsilon_\theta = u/rεθ​=u/r

EN: The distinguishing feature of axisymmetric problems is the hoop (circumferential) strain:

εθ=ur\boxed{\varepsilon_\theta = \frac{u}{r}} εθ​=ru​​

where uuu is the radial displacement and rrr is the radial coordinate. This strain arises because radial expansion/contraction changes the circumference. The axisymmetric strain vector has 4 components (one more than plane problems):

ε={εrεzεθγrz}\boldsymbol{\varepsilon} = \begin{Bmatrix} \varepsilon_r \\ \varepsilon_z \\ \varepsilon_\theta \\ \gamma_{rz} \end{Bmatrix} ε=⎩⎨⎧​εr​εz​εθ​γrz​​⎭⎬⎫​

中文： 轴对称的标志性特征——环向应变 εθ=u/r\varepsilon_\theta=u/rεθ​=u/r。当结构径向变形时，周长改变产生环向应变。轴对称有4个应变分量（比平面问题多了 εθ\varepsilon_\thetaεθ​）。

Q46 ⭐ 3节点轴对称单元不是常应变单元

EN: Although the 3-node axisymmetric element uses the same shape functions as CST in the rrr-zzz plane, it is NOT a constant strain element. The hoop strain εθ=u/r\varepsilon_\theta = u/rεθ​=u/r contains 1/r1/r1/r terms (through the shape function Ni/r=(αi+βir+γiz)/(2Ar)N_i/r = (\alpha_i + \beta_i r + \gamma_i z)/(2Ar)Ni​/r=(αi​+βi​r+γi​z)/(2Ar)), which vary with radial position. Therefore, the B-matrix is not constant, and the strains vary within the element. This is a common exam trap.

中文： ⚠️ 高频易错——3节点轴对称单元虽然在r-z平面看像CST三角形，但不是常应变单元！因为 εθ=u/r\varepsilon_\theta=u/rεθ​=u/r 含有 1/r1/r1/r 项，导致B矩阵随r变化，应变在单元内不是常数。这是与平面CST的本质区别。

Q47 🟡 轴对称D矩阵（4×4）

D=E(1+ν)(1−2ν)[1−ννν0ν1−νν0νν1−ν00001−2ν2]\mathbf{D} = \frac{E}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}\begin{bmatrix} 1-\nu & \nu & \nu & 0 \\ \nu & 1-\nu & \nu & 0 \\ \nu & \nu & 1-\nu & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \frac{1-2\nu}{2} \end{bmatrix}D=(1+ν)(1−2ν)E​​1−ννν0​ν1−νν0​νν1−ν0​00021−2ν​​​

Corresponding stress and strain vectors: {σr,σz,σθ,τrz}T\{\sigma_r, \sigma_z, \sigma_\theta, \tau_{rz}\}^T{σr​,σz​,σθ​,τrz​}T and {εr,εz,εθ,γrz}T\{\varepsilon_r, \varepsilon_z, \varepsilon_\theta, \gamma_{rz}\}^T{εr​,εz​,εθ​,γrz​}T.

中文： 轴对称D矩阵为4×4，含 (1−ν)/((1+ν)(1−2ν))(1-\nu)/((1+\nu)(1-2\nu))(1−ν)/((1+ν)(1−2ν)) 因子（与3D问题类似）。

Q48 🔴 轴对称刚度积分中的 2πr2\pi r2πr

EN: The volume element in axisymmetric coordinates is dV=r dr dθ dzdV = r\,dr\,d\theta\,dzdV=rdrdθdz. Integrating over θ\thetaθ from 0 to 2π2\pi2π gives:

dV=2πr dA(where dA=dr dz is the area in the r-z plane)dV = 2\pi r \, dA \quad \text{(where } dA = dr\,dz \text{ is the area in the r-z plane)} dV=2πrdA(where dA=drdz is the area in the r-z plane)

Therefore, the element stiffness integral for axisymmetric elements is:

ke=∫Ae2πr BTDB dA\boxed{\mathbf{k}_e = \int_{A_e} 2\pi r \, \mathbf{B}^T \mathbf{D} \mathbf{B} \, dA} ke​=∫Ae​​2πrBTDBdA​

This is fundamentally different from plane problems (t dAt\,dAtdA). The rrr inside the integral makes analytical integration difficult, so numerical integration is generally required.

中文： ⚠️ 轴对称的刚度积分中有 2πr2\pi r2πr（而不是平面问题的 ttt）！体积微元 dV=2πr dAdV = 2\pi r\,dAdV=2πrdA。这个额外的 rrr 因子使得即使B矩阵近似常数，积分也不是简单的面积乘积，通常需要数值积分。

Q49 🔴 轴对称与平面CST的全面对比

EN:

Feature Plane CST 3-Node Axisymmetric
Coordinate system xxx-yyy (Cartesian) rrr-zzz (Cylindrical)
Strain components 3 (εx,εy,γxy\varepsilon_x, \varepsilon_y, \gamma_{xy}εx​,εy​,γxy​) 4 (adds εθ\varepsilon_\thetaεθ​)
Hoop strain None εθ=u/r\varepsilon_\theta = u/rεθ​=u/r
D-matrix 3×3 4×4
Strain variation Constant NOT constant (1/r1/r1/r terms)
Volume element t dAt\,dAtdA 2πr dA2\pi r\,dA2πrdA
Numerical integration Not needed for CST Generally required

中文： 这个对比表是必背内容。最关键的区别：轴对称有环向应变，应变不是常数，积分含 2πr2\pi r2πr。
